Common questions

Does Detroit's rental-certificate procedure apply in Mount Clemens?

No. Mount Clemens is in Macomb County with its own requirements. Bring the current use, proposed use and any agency notice to the first conversation.

I am converting a commercial space to apartments. Which lead rules apply?

It depends on the final use. A residential conversion can bring federal renovation rules into play, while commercial-only space follows different worker-protection requirements. Ask before work starts.
