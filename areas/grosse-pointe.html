Common questions

An assessment found intact lead paint. Do I need abatement?

Not necessarily. Intact coatings on low-wear surfaces are a different situation from friction or impact surfaces. The assessment should distinguish them and recommend targeted controls.

How is Grosse Pointe different from Grosse Pointe Park for permits?

They are separate municipalities, along with the Farms, the Shores and the Woods. Name the exact city so the project is not checked against a neighbor's rules.
