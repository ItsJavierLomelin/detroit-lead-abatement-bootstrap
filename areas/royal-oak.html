Common questions

I am renovating before a move-in date. Where does lead testing fit?

Before demolition. Testing or an assessment first keeps the crew from disturbing lead paint blind, and it keeps the project from creating the dust problem it was meant to fix.

Is Royal Oak under Detroit's rental lead rules?

No. Royal Oak is a separate city with its own building authority. Ask the local office about permits, and ask the lead professional about credentials and final documentation.
